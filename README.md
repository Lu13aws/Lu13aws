### What I'm building

**[AI Knowledge Platform](https://github.com/Lu13aws/ai-platform-project-v1)** —
a modular RAG platform on AWS. Scheduled Lambda agents collect public signals for
three radars (technology, competitors, regulation), classify them with an LLM,
store them in PostgreSQL + pgvector and answer questions with cited sources.
Includes a corporate prototype with Cognito RBAC, audit logging and GDPR Art. 17
deletion — plus a documented compliance layer (NIST AI RMF, GDPR/DSG, AWS
Well-Architected) that states honestly what is and isn't in place.

**AI Consulting Accelerator** — the consulting process itself as a tool:
Discovery → Analysis → Delivery, 17 named and versioned skills grounded in IREB,
BABOK, BPMN and PMBOK, every output cited and explicitly marked as a draft for
human review. Quality measured by an eval harness with 24 golden cases.

Live demo: **[platform.bridging-data.com](https://platform.bridging-data.com)**
